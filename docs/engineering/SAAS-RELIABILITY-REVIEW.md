# SaaS reliability review

Reviewed 17 November 2026 using the user-invoked grilling skill, with fresh-context read-only reviews of runtime, operations and account/customer experience. Scope: PRD, engineering plan, workflow, task records, local skills and repository inventory. Q1–Q58 remain accepted. This document records planning gaps and recommendations; it does not claim implemented controls, observed production defects or runtime certification.

## Assessment

The architecture and product rules are coherent. Existing strengths include committed server state, immutable submissions, scoped permissions, explicit media grants/revocation, payment reconciliation, durable email intents, accessible recovery states, synthetic evidence, and gated restore/rollback/media tests. The repository contains planning files and a historical simulated mockup, not an application whose reliability can yet be measured.

The main weakness is that some operational criteria say to prove recovery or capacity without defining the cross-service failure cases. A successful single workshop, fresh-browser smoke test or database restore would not by itself establish a stable multi-customer service. Resolve the engineering findings below in the existing tasks; no extra service fleet or additional paid infrastructure is implied.

## Accepted decisions Q59-Q61

The user accepted Q59 with a maximum of three simultaneous workshops per workspace, and accepted Q60/Q61 unchanged.

| Question | Accepted answer and consequence |
|---|---|
| Q59 — simultaneous workshops | Yes, maximum three live workshops per workspace initially. Prove all three at the published session configuration plus cross-workspace concurrency. Started sessions still count through activity pauses/disconnection; drafts/ended sessions do not. Atomic admission rejects a fourth start and separately enforces verified global/provider headroom. No unlimited hosting or increased spending is implied. |
| Q60 — privileged account protection | Yes: require a second authentication factor for the platform operator, workspace owners and users with billing/data-administration authority, with secure recovery and reauthentication for sensitive actions. Free guest participants do not acquire an account/MFA requirement. Ordinary facilitator-only MFA need not be compulsory initially. |
| Q61 — initial support commitment | Yes: publish support hours for the initial paid service, provide named live incident coverage for scheduled pilot sessions, and show incident/contact information. Do not imply a 24/7 response guarantee. The actual owner, hours/timezone, escalation and contact destination must be configured and rehearsed before the applicable gate. |
