# Debrio

Debrio supports facilitated workshops and human-run simulations through shared agendas, group activities and scoped resources. These terms capture distinctions already established in the product plan.

## Language

**Platform owner**:
The person operating Debrio, with permanent free use of the designated owner workspace; this is distinct from owning a customer workspace.
_Avoid_: Every workspace owner, participant, universal content administrator

**Workspace owner**:
The single primary owner of a workspace, supported by administrators with explicitly delegated permissions; ownership can transfer to an accepting recipient and does not confer platform-owner benefits.
_Avoid_: Equal co-owner, platform owner, automatic facilitator

**Workspace**:
A container for a customer's workshop definitions, sessions and subscription, usable by an individual or an organisation. A person may belong to multiple workspaces, each with separate membership permissions and content.
_Avoid_: Build/Run/Review area, participant screen, registered company

**Personal workspace**:
A workspace owned by an individual trainer or consultant, who can use Debrio without a company or team.
_Avoid_: Participant account, separate product, solo pricing tier

**Workshop definition**:
A reusable workshop with a stable identity whose versions describe objectives, activities and their configuration, from which sessions are run.
_Avoid_: Session, live workshop

**Workshop version**:
A specific edition of a workshop definition's agenda and configuration, editable as a draft until the facilitator marks it Ready to run. A ready version is frozen; subsequent edits belong to a new draft version.
_Avoid_: Live session state, publication approval

**Session**:
One delivery of a workshop definition with its own participants, progress and work in a hosting workspace; consultant-delivered client sessions normally belong to the consultant's workspace.
_Avoid_: Workshop definition, template

**Group**:
A set of participants assigned to work together for an activity or round; a jigsaw may preserve both home-group and expert-group assignments.
_Avoid_: Media room

**Participant session identity**:
A person's stable identity within one session, to which their work and assignments belong. It links to their authenticated account when they join signed in; guests can participate without an account.
_Avoid_: Display name, browser connection, workspace membership, cross-session learner profile

**Media room**:
A main-room or breakout conversation space whose membership is distinct from group membership and resource access.
_Avoid_: Group, workspace

**Resource assignment**:
An allocation of a particular resource to individuals, groups or roles, subject to its release and access conditions.
_Avoid_: Room membership

**Attempt**:
One submitted response in a practice sequence, owned by either one participant or one group; a retry is a separate, linked attempt. Group attempts preserve their contributor roster at submission independently of who acts as recorder.
_Avoid_: Draft, overwritten answer, recorder-owned group work

**Recorder**:
The designated group member authorized to submit the group's structured draft; other assigned members may contribute, and the facilitator may replace the recorder without changing authorship or losing work.
_Avoid_: Sole contributor, recording service

**Client summary**:
A curated read-only session summary shared with an explicitly invited client contact, containing only material approved for that audience.
_Avoid_: Workspace access, unrestricted session record

**Aggregate-only poll**:
A poll whose results expose no named responses to users or exports; internal participant identity may still prevent duplicate responses.
_Avoid_: Untraceable anonymous poll

**Publication approval**:
An author's permission to share an identifiable individual item at a specific version with a specified audience.
_Avoid_: Blanket consent, session membership
