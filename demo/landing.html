Every group, back in one room.

Debrio brings your workshops together. Breakout rooms, timed activities, questions and evidence live in one session, so you can teach instead of juggling tools.

Explore demo See how a session runs

Build it once. Run it live. Keep what was learned.

  1. 1

    Build

    Design the session as steps: the prompt, the timing, the output you expect and the materials each group receives.

  2. 2

    Run

    Let people in from the lobby, open breakouts, watch every room at once and move everyone to the next step together.

  3. 3

    Review

    Compare first and revised attempts, choose the evidence for your debrief and export a record of what each group produced.
